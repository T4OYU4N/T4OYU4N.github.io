Welcome! I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in the [Department of Systems Engineering and Engineering Management (SEEM)](https://www.se.cuhk.edu.hk/) at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/index.html) supervised by [Prof. Huifu Xu](https://www1.se.cuhk.edu.hk/~hfxu/). Previously, I received my M.S. in Management Science and Engineering and B.S. in Statistics from [Nanjing University (NJU)](https://www.nju.edu.cn/en/) in 2023 and 2020, respectively, supervised by [Prof. Caihua Chen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=I6ewvyIAAAAJ).

Email: yuantao at link dot cuhk dot edu dot hk


# Research Interests

My research interests lie in operations research and optimization, particularly in decision-making under uncertainty and equilibrium problems with incomplete information.
